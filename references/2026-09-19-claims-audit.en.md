# Jev catalog: audit of claims and supporting evidence

[简体中文](2026-09-19-claims-audit.md) | **English**

Completed **2026-09-19 11:30:00 Beijing time (UTC+08:00)**. Covers all **106 cases in 11 categories** at catalog commit `2027762`. Every main-post text was rechecked, with targeted primary documentation/source reviews. **None of the applications was independently run for this audit. Missing evidence is not evidence of fabrication.** The classification evaluates claims, not authors’ honesty.

| Assessment | Cases | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [A · Clearer support for bounded function/mechanism](#tier-a) | 18 | Inspectable implementation, explicit inputs/outputs or clearly bounded findings; not a validation of every performance figure or production readiness |
| [B · Plausible demo, unverified effectiveness](#tier-b) | 78 | Mostly videos, isolated runs or private evaluations; insufficient to establish general accuracy or costs, but also insufficient to label the claim overstated |
| [C · Specific claims exceed their evidence](#tier-c) | 10 | Absolute guarantees, broad extrapolation, unsupported comparative superiority, or claims inconsistent with the project’s own documentation; not a declaration that the entire project is fake |

The largest gaps concern attribution to Jev alone, long-term reliability and equal-quality speed/cost comparisons. A displayed 99% score is not 99% test accuracy; 500 characters do not establish 500 per-frame inference loops; discarding 94% of context does not establish preservation of all useful information.

## Assessment rules

- Inspect the specific claim. An output, implementation and independently validated outcome are different evidence levels. Likes are an inclusion threshold, not credibility.
- Separate components: the API returns choices, scores or yes/no judgments, while surrounding code observes, acts and composes results. [Official introduction](https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction)
- Confidence summarizes candidate probabilities; it is not a measured accuracy rate for the task. [Confidence documentation](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence)
- The official Jev 1.13 limitations cover numerical precision, indirect reasoning, adversarial input and chained text generation. They apply to that version and do not prove a composed application must fail. [Version-specific limitations](https://docs.typesafe.ai/model-jaggedness/jev-1.13)
- Compare matched inputs, quality criteria and timing boundaries, including preprocessing, other models, retries and execution costs.

## Important distinctions

- **Tax classification:** bounded zero-misclassification findings coexist with 38 low-confidence failures in a separate blank-form set. This is not universal tax-document accuracy. [Pinned documentation](https://github.com/kyotofin/tax-doc-classifier/blob/6afcf701395466d7c936ec8178daf017b9d96b0c/README.md)
- **Browser Use / WebMCP:** 49/49 tasks covered across three attempts each differs from 141/147 successful attempts. [Benchmark](https://webmcp.com/benchmark)
- **Script.it:** the author discloses 75% confirmed-bug recall; lower cost is not an equal-recall comparison. [Source post](https://x.com/liorshkiller/status/2100936106615140757)
- **Probably / shell history:** cached hosted playback and fabricated demo history are explicitly disclosed, not proof of fabrication or everyday effectiveness. [Probably](https://probably-lang.southpolesteve.workers.dev) · [shell documentation](https://github.com/mrnugget/jev-shell-history/blob/4b2b75d26c0ccf5726263904514a22a8e11659ea/README.md)
- **Cua status correction:** #3916 is now merged; #3943 implements an optional visual extension but remains a draft. Old unmerged-status notes cannot establish present nonexistence, and a merge does not certify universal cross-platform computer use. [#3916](https://github.com/trycua/cua/pull/3916) · [#3943](https://github.com/trycua/cua/pull/3943)
