## What the wider search changed

The previous 11-case selection did not cover enough leads. This pass revisited 101 uncollected leads and all 349 X cards across five public [JEVLAB](https://jev-lab.com/en/?platform=x&scope=global) index pages, using [MadeWithJev](https://madewithjev.com/) and the existing inbox as discovery inputs. Index cards are leads, not evidence of independent applications. After source-ID deduplication against the catalog, the pool contained 302 candidates: 301 snapshots retrieved, one unavailable, and 118 meeting both ≥200 likes and attached-media criteria. Following a Halite quote found one additional original, making **303 source posts in the screening ledger**. Supporting replies/articles outside this pool are linked per case, not counted as additional applications.

**8 of the 35 new cards have original posts after September 21, 11:02:26 Beijing time; 27 are older omissions recovered in this pass.** These are new catalog entries, not 35 newly released products. Public indexes have coverage and freshness limits; this is not an exhaustive X export. FxTwitter metrics may be cached. We reviewed source text, metadata and selected author documentation/code; we did not independently run these projects or verify every video frame.
