## Use and value

Score children’s snacks in batches to organize a product catalog.

**Useful aspect (analysis):** Focuses on batch product organization rather than estimating a meal, as in Calorie Notebook. Separate criteria can be inspected more easily than a single overall score.

## Inputs, steps and outputs

The author reports using Jev to score 3,000 snacks on multiple criteria. The site offers sugar-based browsing, but that does not establish that Jev computes every nutritional grade.

Undisclosed prompts, state formats, thresholds and recovery logic remain unknown. Inclusion of a demo does not establish a stable release.
