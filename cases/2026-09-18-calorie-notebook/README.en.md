## Use and value

Write down what you ate and receive quick calorie, nutrient and total values in the interface.

**Useful aspect (analysis):** Emphasizes feedback while entering food, potentially reducing item-by-item logging effort. Compare responsiveness with the predictive spreadsheet, while separately checking portion interpretation and numerical evidence.
