# Webpage and feed filtering: how they work and compare

[简体中文](2026-09-18-filter.md) | **English**

Collected / source-reviewed: 2026-09-18. Status: **not independently reproduced**.

## Choosing an approach

The X filter targets posts in a feed; Unclutter targets ads, banners and upsells across webpages. One classifies content, the other page elements, so their rules cannot necessarily be reused directly. Sponsor Skip targets spoken sponsorship along a video timeline rather than webpage elements; caption availability, transcription and boundary errors are distinct comparison dimensions.

## Individual comparisons

These are analytical strengths and limitations based on public descriptions, not controlled experimental findings.

## Workflow and mechanism

Extract posts/elements → apply user-written semantic rules through Jev → hide, collapse or remove.

Filtering depends on extraction and rule scope. Fast inference does not guarantee no flicker, false removals or broken interactions; page structures can change.

Individual input and implementation differences are documented in the linked cases. This is an application-level synthesis, not a claim that every implementation is identical or an account of Jev’s internal training architecture.

## Suggested reproduction experiments

Compare false removals, missed targets, initial display latency and per-page cost on a fixed page set. Check normal dialogs, login, infinite scroll and undo behavior.

**Not run.** There are no timing, accuracy or cost results produced by this repository.
